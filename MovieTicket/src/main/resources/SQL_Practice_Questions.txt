SQL PRACTICE QUESTIONS
Based on schema: customer(customerId, customerName, customerEmail, customerCity)
              movies(movieId, movieName, genre, ticketPrice)
              bookings(bookingId, bookingDate, seats, totalAmount, customerId, movieId)

-- BASICS --
1. Display all customer records.
2. Display all movies with their genre and ticket price.
3. Display all bookings sorted by bookingDate in descending order.
4. Display customer name and city for customers living in "Pune".
5. Display movie names where genre is "Action".
6. Display all bookings where seats booked is greater than 2.
7. Display all distinct cities from the customer table.
8. Display all distinct genres from the movies table.
9. Display top 5 bookings with the highest totalAmount.
10. Display customer names in alphabetical order.

-- FILTERING & CONDITIONS --
11. Display customers whose email ends with "@gmail.com".
12. Display movies with ticket price between 200 and 400.
13. Display bookings made in the last 30 days.
14. Display customers whose name starts with "A".
15. Display movies whose name contains the word "2" (e.g. sequels).
16. Display bookings where totalAmount is greater than 1500.
17. Display customers from "Pune" or "Mumbai".
18. Display bookings with seats between 1 and 3.
19. Display movies that are NOT of genre "Comedy-Horror".
20. Display customers whose city is NULL or not specified.

-- AGGREGATE FUNCTIONS --
21. Display the total number of customers.
22. Display the total number of bookings.
23. Display the total revenue generated from all bookings.
24. Display the average ticket price of all movies.
25. Display the maximum and minimum totalAmount from bookings.
26. Display the average number of seats booked per booking.
27. Display the total number of distinct movies booked.
28. Display the total number of distinct customers who made a booking.
29. Display the highest priced movie's name and price.
30. Display the total revenue generated per genre.

-- GROUP BY & HAVING --
31. Display total booking amount per customer.
32. Display customer details whose total booking amount is greater than 1500.
33. Display number of bookings made by each customer.
34. Display movie name, genre, number of bookings per movie, total distinct customers who booked
    that movie, and total revenue generated by that movie.
35. Display cities having more than 2 customers.
36. Display genres having total revenue greater than 2000.
37. Display customers who have made more than 1 booking.
38. Display movies that have been booked by more than 2 distinct customers.
39. Display the average totalAmount spent per city.
40. Display the total seats booked per movie.

-- JOINS --
41. Display customer name along with the movies they booked.
42. Display movie name along with the customer name and city who booked it.
43. Display booking details (date, seats, amount) along with customer name and movie name.
44. Display customers who have never made a booking (use LEFT JOIN).
45. Display movies that have never been booked (use LEFT JOIN).
46. Display customer name, movie name, and totalAmount for bookings made in the current month.

-- SUBQUERIES --
47. Display the customer(s) who spent the maximum total amount overall.
48. Display movies whose ticket price is greater than the average ticket price of all movies.
49. Display customers who booked the most expensive movie.
50. Display the movie(s) with the highest total revenue among all movies.
